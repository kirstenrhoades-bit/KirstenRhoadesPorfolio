My approach

Less content.
More experience.

I combine ADDIE and SAM approaches with adult learning principles, experiential learning and visual storytelling. I establish measurable learning objectives using Bloom’s Taxonomy, collaborate with SMEs to prioritise essential knowledge, and design meaningful practice and formative and summative assessment. Learner feedback informs continuous improvement.
