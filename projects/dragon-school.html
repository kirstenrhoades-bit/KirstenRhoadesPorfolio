CASE STUDY · Dragon School — Consultation Practice

Choosing the right intervention

Collaborated on a simulated consultation and learning needs analysis exercise focused on a fictional dragon-rider academy. This was a design challenge myself and other designers in my squad set fro our wider team. We wanted to focus on learning consultation, needs analysis and thinking outside of the box.

Consultation → Analysis → Learning Strategy
Project focus
Learning designSME collaborationVisual storytellingApplication

Design challenge & rationale

The exercise explored declining assessment performance, limited practical exposure, inconsistent assessment and restricted technology access. The proposed approach emphasised tactile journals, scenario missions and standardised observation.

Learning experience & evaluation

This was a collaborative practice exercise, not an implemented programme. The outcomes and target measures are design proposals, not achieved results.

Consultation artefact

This collaborative practice exercise explored a performance gap, learner preferences, environmental constraints and assessment consistency. The proposed response used tactile resources, scenario-based missions and standardised observation rather than defaulting to another digital course. It was a training exercise, not an implemented programme.
