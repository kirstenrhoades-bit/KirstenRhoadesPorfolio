CASE STUDY · Ground Stability

Making safety concepts visible

Designed and developed the complete learning experience in Articulate Storyline, from operational SME discussions and content curation through storyboard, interactions, After Effects animations and a Storyline question bank.

Project focus
Learning designSME collaborationVisual storytellingApplication

Design challenge & rationale

The challenge was not the absence of documentation; it was inconsistent adherence to an existing process. The learning approach made underlying safety principles and the possible consequences of decisions visible rather than simply repeating written instructions.

Learning experience & evaluation

The module uses visual explanations and scenario-based assessment to connect concepts with real-world judgement. Learner feedback is invited at the end of the experience. Measured changes in compliance have not been provided.

Supporting animation

My role and ownership

I designed and developed the CBT in Articulate Storyline, collaborating directly with ground crew and subject matter experts to prioritise content and plan the storyboard. I created the visual explanation animations in Adobe After Effects, built the interactions and developed a Storyline question bank with scenario-based questions. Feedback is requested at the end of the module.

From storyboard to experience

The original storyboard maps concepts, decision points, warning indicators and assessment questions before development. The animation translates abstract principles into visual cause and effect. The case study presents selected media; it does not claim a measured improvement in compliance.
