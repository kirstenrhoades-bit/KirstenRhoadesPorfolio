Why video?

Video is a great way to share ideas, show process and connect with a wider audience when delivering the importance of a concept. Good videos need to be quick, engaging and have clear audio. They need to be inclusive and help bolster your point in learning. Video is most effective as a state change and a way to introduce a topic or idea.
