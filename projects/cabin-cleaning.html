CASE STUDY · Cabin Cleaning — Rise + 360° POV

Learning from the learner’s perspective

Developed a pre-induction Articulate Rise module supported by instructional video and 360° point-of-view footage, helping new starters see a working environment before practical training.

Project focus

Design challenge & rationale

The Rise recording demonstrates structured topic navigation, bite-sized explanations and embedded operational footage. This updated version is used in place of the earlier generic cabin-cleaning recording.

Learning experience & evaluation

The design combines clear explanations with video demonstrations to support preparation before induction. No independent performance outcomes have been supplied.

Additional 360° instructional example

Alongside the Rise module, I created learner-perspective footage showing an economy-seat cleaning sequence. The 360° approach helps new starters see the environment and task before their in-person induction.
